How to use it

Look once, then choose the box that matches the cover’s visual appeal.

  1. Drag the image into any 1–6 box.
  2. Or press 1–6 to score immediately.
  3. Drag between boxes to change a score; use Undo to go back.
  4. When all covers are scored, export the result as CSV.

Scale: 1 = clearly weak · 3 = ordinary · 6 = very appealing and worth opening.
